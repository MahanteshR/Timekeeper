TIMEKEEPER 0.7.1

Your data.
Your device.

This page describes the 0.7.1 Android beta and this download website.

Updated 29 October 2026

What stays on your device

Timekeeper stores your selected apps, timer rules, preferences and recorded usage history locally. Version 0.7.1 has no account system, advertising or analytics and does not request Android’s INTERNET permission. The app does not send your usage history to a Timekeeper server.

Why permissions are requested

AccessPurpose in Timekeeper
AccessibilityIdentify foreground app/window changes and display the blocking interface when a limit or cooldown applies.
Usage accessRead Android app-usage history for the dashboard and reconcile foreground usage.
NotificationsOptionally show remaining time while a limited app is active.
A backup folder you selectCreate, verify and restore timer configuration files using Android’s document picker.

Accessibility is a powerful system permission. The service declares access to interactive windows; Android may describe broad screen-access capabilities in its permission prompt. Its purpose in Timekeeper is foreground detection and blocking. Review that prompt and grant access only if you are comfortable with it. You can revoke these permissions in Android Settings.

Optional backups

Backups include your app identifiers, labels, timer settings and preferences. They do not include usage history, live cooldowns or permission grants. Timekeeper does not encrypt the backup documents. Choose a private folder and review files before sharing them.

If you choose a cloud-backed document provider, that provider may upload or sync the files through its own account and privacy settings. Disconnecting backup leaves previously saved copies in the chosen folder.

Deleting your data

Clearing Timekeeper’s app storage or uninstalling it removes its local app data. Exported backup documents remain in your selected folder until you delete them there. Android permission grants and recorded runtime history are not restored from a timer backup.

Website and download hosting

This site has no analytics scripts, advertising, account forms or tracking cookies added by Timekeeper. Its fonts, illustrations and scripts are served with the site. The public deployment is intended for GitHub Pages, with the APK hosted alongside the website.

GitHub handles requests to a GitHub-hosted site and may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under its privacy statement. The hosting provider can see a download request; that does not give it access to the app’s usage history.

Beta status and questions

Version 0.7.1 is a debug-signed beta. A separate security/release review and broader compatibility testing remain pending. This notice describes the current release; future releases may require an updated notice.

During this early beta, use the person who shared Timekeeper with you as your support contact. Do not send passwords, banking details or unredacted usage histories in feedback.

Choosing whether to install? Read the setup and limitations guide before enabling permissions.
