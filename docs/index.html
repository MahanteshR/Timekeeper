BEFORE YOU TRY IT

A few good
questions.

Is Timekeeper free?+

Yes. The 0.7.1 public beta is free to try, including intervals, cooldowns, strict mode and backup/restore. No payment or account is needed.

Do I need Shizuku or root?+

No. Version 0.7.1 uses Android Accessibility and Usage access. It does not include Shizuku, wireless debugging or root setup.

Will it work on my phone?+

The APK requires Android 12 or later. Testing so far has focused on the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra. Other Android devices are welcome to try the beta, but manufacturer behavior, layouts and background restrictions still need testing.

Why does it need Accessibility?+

Timekeeper uses window changes to identify the active app and show a blocking screen when its allowance or session ends. Accessibility is powerful access: review Android’s permission prompt before enabling it. Some banking or security-sensitive apps may object while Accessibility services are enabled.

Does it completely stop the restricted app?+

Version 0.7.1 displays a blocking screen. A restricted app may appear briefly when opened, and background playback or services can continue. It does not suspend or force-stop other apps. Strict mode locks rule changes inside Timekeeper; it cannot make Android permissions or uninstall controls unavailable.

What does a backup include?+

Your app timer rules and preferences. Usage history, live cooldowns and permission grants are not included. Backup files identify your selected apps and are not encrypted by Timekeeper, so choose a private location. Settings → Back up and restore explains what is saved.
